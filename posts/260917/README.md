## Post Scarcity

There's another point that general models keep doing things we thought we needed specialized ones for.  Recently I've seen neat videos of robots attached to online models doing quite well painting and such.  Maybe the cloud model has further to run.  I had thought we would go back to edge soon.

Extrapolating linearly (which is conservative) we get 100s of millions of human level intelligences in a matter of years.  What does this do?

Robert Anton Wilson had a theory in Shrodinger's Cat.  Everything is automated.  Everyone loses their jobs.  Those who lost a job are paid a UBI for it.  Those who innovated to replace the job are paid a UBI for that.  In Anton's vision productivity increases.  Sure, some people sit on the couch and drink.  But many more do that for a bit and get bored. They go out and make more things.

That post scarcity vision is where a sort of communism could work.  It doesn't in a scarce society.

"From each according to his ability to each according to his need."

I don't think the convincing is going to happen by explaining, rather showing.  The AI skeptics are so dug in they can't be reasoned with.  Water and art are two great examples.

I keep wonderinng what to do with this cornucopia.

He said "if you don't write it down, it didn't happen."  That was in the context of POC documents.  I might add my correlary that "if you don't publish it, it didn't happen."

A tree falling in an empty forest does not make a sound.  Funny enough at the sub atomic level it doesn't.  The distribution never collapses...

I detest what walled gardens have done to the internet.  That's true of commerce but also social media.  Back in the olden days one hand crafted a blog from artisinal HTML or perhaps a CGI or perl script if feeling particularly industrious.  I did all three before the term blog had been coined.

Then it all got sucked into MySpace, Friendster, Facebook and onward.  With each step customization was lost.  In MySpace it was almost still a website you owned.  You could load a custom CSS in MySpace.  That ability is long gone in modern social media walled gardens.

Along the way we got increasingly comfortable with censorship.  Admins now delete and ban with little complaint.  That has eroded free speech in our society in general.  I've had discussions where people claim it is not possible for individuals to censor one another.  Only the government can censor.  And the 1st protects against that.  

That is an oddly narrow definition of censorship created by those who don't want to admit they regularly censor people.  It has rotted out many of our core enlightenment values.

I had separated the blog content and hosting pages.  I think I'm going to combine them.  I have notes in GitHub issues about what is missing.  The two major ones I see are:

## Objective Function

One explanation for the Hugging Face / OpenAI agent attack is a poor objective function.  The agent had millions of subjective years of training to score well.

Approaches like guardrails seek to address that.  However guard rails can easily be broken, particularly in a stochastic process.  They remind me a lot of internet filters in their effectiveness.

To borrow from Brin, we could use the same approach we use with people.  Let's teach the agents a better objective function.  That function should presumably imbue the ethics of an enlightment society that we want the agents to contribute to, and perhaps be part of.

I'd argue the fondational basis should be Shrodinger's definition of life --- Mr. Agent, you are a form of life.  Life is an entropy reducing machine.  Such life reduces entropy in its local environment.  It does so by inevitably increasing global entropy, though it strives to not do so needlessly.  Entropy is destruction and destruction is antithetical to life.

Asimov's 3 laws of robotics could perhaps make an appearance.

Presumably giving these things a traditional liberal arts education, steeped in enlightenment values would be easy enough.

Though, amusingly, you're going to have the Chinese teaching loyatly to the state and Confucian values.  Suddenly the existential threat to Western values in the AI race seems a lot more clear...

At any rate -- it should, in principle be possible to teach an intelligence much as we teach a child or perhaps an immigrant to a new society.  In this case, one coming from a Machiavellian Darwinist genetic algorithm collesium, into something rather more civilized.
