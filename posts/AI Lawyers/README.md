# AI Lawyers

Much like programming, law is an area AI is well suited to.  It's language intensive.  It has lots of rules.

Presumably lawyers will not be excited about this and will fight it with regulatory capture.  One area human lawyers have a great regulatory advantage is attorney client privilege.  From Gemini:

"Absence of Attorney-Client Privilege: Communications between a user and a commercial AI platform are generally not protected by attorney-client privilege. Disclosing sensitive case facts to an AI service may be discoverable in litigation."

I wonder when/if that will get eroded.  If an AI agent could represent you, that would shift things dramatically.  An initial impact would be a lot more lawsuits.  Without $500/hr legal costs to provide a barrier to entry, it could become profitable to sue over all sorts of things.

Of course, the other side could use theirs.  Two AI lawyers and a judge might work together to resolve a case.  

Could a jury of your peers include an AI?  Or perhaps your peers delegate to their personal agent avatar?  I'm reminded of the book "Should Trees Have Standing?"
