# 261122 - The Perfect Place

One of my favorite activities for a quiet Sunday afternoon is agonizing over where I ought to live.  The main consideration is where will be best for the kids.

An advantage of our current house in Tacoma is an enormous amount of space.  Our house in Boulder had much less.  I loved building things, but it was hard there.  

Previously we'd lived in a larger house in Colorado Springs.  That had lots of interior space.  But it also had a sizeable two car garage with tool closets.  There was a large driveway outside as well.  The space was conducize to making num chuks out of scrap wood and assembling model rockets.

To contrast, the Boulder house had no garage.  In my time there, I turned primarily to more intellectual pursuits, reading an enormous amount, role playing games and such.  When, in college, I wanted to assemble bike and weld, some of that work moved into the living room.  Other work took place in the yard or at the CU Physics shop.

I keep thinking one moat against AI is actually building things.  Yes, maybe humanoid robots will replace plumbers and all.  But, perhaps a space will remain.  That at least seems more likely than us beating AI in some well defined virtual space such as coding.

As such, actually knowing how to wood work or weld seems useful.  Gardening is nice too.  This is the slippery slope to buying acerage and moving into the woods of course.  Of current situation seems a reasonable middle ground.
