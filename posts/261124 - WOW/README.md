I keep thinking about what a better social network would be.  Part of the driver is the wild amount of censorship I've observed on existing platforms.  Nextdoor and Reddit are the most eggregious.

Some points:

* **Dencentralization** - One key part would be decentralization.  That way a single bad actor could not torpedo the network.  Enshittification could not happen.
* **E Drugs** - I think Reddit proves that a Tik Tok style feed is not neccesary, even today for a succesful social network.
* **Static and Open** - Especially with the rise of AI, static sites have become even more important.  I think having renderred HTML or at least MD that AIs can ingest is important.  More and more people are consuming information that way.  Enable it, don't fight it.

One thought I've had is to break down the barrier between post and comment.  Instead, just have a node and relationship.  That may have been influenced by a half decade of working with Neo4j...

Graphs of posts might even cross social network instances.

In such a system, you could censor your own isntance, but people could still reply on theirs.  A crawler might then weight posts across the network using some mixture of Dalio's believability weighted decision making and Doctorow's Whuffie score.
